Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how Epic Forge Games uses cookies and similar technologies on our platform.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help improve functionality, remember preferences, and enhance overall user experience.

2. How We Use Cookies

Epic Forge Games uses cookies to ensure the website functions properly and to improve performance.

  • To remember user preferences and settings
  • To analyze website traffic and usage patterns
  • To enhance security and prevent misuse
  • To improve loading speed and performance
  • To provide a better and more personalized experience

3. Types of Cookies We Use

We may use session cookies (temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser) and persistent cookies (cookies that remain on your device for a set period).

4. Third-Party Cookies

Some features may use third-party services that place their own cookies. We do not control these cookies and recommend reviewing the privacy policies of those services.

5. Managing Cookies

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. However, disabling cookies may affect certain features and functionality of the website.

6. Updates to This Policy

Epic Forge Games may update this Cookie Policy at any time. Continued use of the website indicates acceptance of the current version.

7. Contact Information

If you have questions about our use of cookies, please contact us through our official communication channels.
