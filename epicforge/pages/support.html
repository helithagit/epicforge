Help Center

Welcome to the Epic Forge Help Center. Find answers to common questions and learn how to get the most out of your gaming experience.

1. Getting Started

New to Epic Forge? Here's everything you need to know to get started:

  • Visit the Epic Forge homepage and browse available games by genre
  • Click on any game card to launch and play instantly — no epicforge required
  • Use the search bar at the top to find a specific game quickly
  • Create a free account to save your progress and preferences

2. Account & Login Issues

Having trouble signing in or managing your account? Try the following:

  • Make sure your email and password are entered correctly
  • Use the "Forgot Password" option on the login page to reset your password
  • Clear your browser cache and cookies, then try again
  • If your account has been locked, contact us via the Contact Us section below

3. Game Not Loading?

If a game isn't loading properly, here are some steps to fix it:

  • Refresh the page and try again
  • Make sure your browser is up to date — we recommend Chrome or Edge
  • Disable any browser extensions or ad blockers that may interfere
  • Check your internet connection speed
  • Try opening the game in an incognito/private window

4. Reporting a Bug

Found something that doesn't work as expected? We appreciate your help in making Epic Forge better.

  • Note the game name, what happened, and what you expected to happen
  • Include your browser name and version if possible
  • Send a detailed report to us through the Contact Us section below

5. Privacy & Data

Epic Forge takes your privacy seriously. For full details on how we handle your data, please refer to our Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy.
