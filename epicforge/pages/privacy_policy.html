Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how Epic Forge Games collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

Epic Forge Games may collect basic information such as your name, email address, device details, and usage data when you interact with our platform.

2. How We Use Information

The information collected is used to improve user experience, maintain platform performance, and provide relevant updates and support.

  • To operate and maintain our services
  • To improve website functionality and performance
  • To respond to user inquiries and support requests
  • To enhance security and prevent misuse
  • To communicate important updates

3. Cookies and Tracking

Epic Forge Games may use cookies and similar technologies to analyze traffic, remember user preferences, and improve overall experience.

4. Data Protection

We implement reasonable security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

5. Third-Party Services

Some features may rely on third-party services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites or services linked from our platform.

6. Data Sharing

Epic Forge Games does not sell or trade your personal information. Information may only be shared when required by law or to protect our rights and security.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the website means you accept the current version of the policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our official channels.
